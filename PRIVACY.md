# Privacy policy

_Last updated: 15 November 2026_

Turnotto is a static web app that runs entirely in your browser.

**What we collect:** nothing. There is no account, no server-side database, no analytics, no advertising, no cookies and no tracking. The app makes no requests to third-party services: fonts and every other file are served from the same site.

**Where your data lives:** the data you enter (names, hours, absences, plans, settings) is stored only on your device, in the browser's local storage, and in the backup or Excel files you choose to save. It never leaves your device unless you share those files yourself.

**Hosting:** the app is hosted on GitHub Pages. As with any website, GitHub may process technical data such as your IP address to serve the pages, under the [GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

**Deleting your data:** in the app go to *Rules → Delete everything*, or clear the site data in your browser.

**Data about other people:** if you enter data about colleagues or employees, you are responsible for doing so in compliance with the law that applies to you (for example the GDPR).

**Changes:** updates to this policy are published in this repository and shown in the app (Info & privacy).

### Informativa privacy (italiano)
Turnotto non raccoglie dati: nessun account, nessun server, nessuna statistica, nessun cookie e nessuna richiesta a servizi di terzi. I dati inseriti restano solo nel tuo browser e nei file che scegli di salvare. L'hosting è su GitHub Pages, che può trattare dati tecnici come l'indirizzo IP secondo la propria informativa. Puoi cancellare tutto da *Regole → Cancella tutto*. Se inserisci dati di altre persone, sei responsabile del rispetto della normativa applicabile (es. GDPR).
